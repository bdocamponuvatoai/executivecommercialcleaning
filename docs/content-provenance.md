the original design concept. They do not depict actual staff, clients or jobs. Wherever they

## Editorial content

Service outlines, cleaning priorities and scheduling options are discussion starting points, not
contractual inclusions. The site tells visitors to confirm scope, availability, methods,
specialized needs and pricing with the team. No warranties, health outcomes, disinfectant claims,
emergency availability, certifications, testimonials, ratings or statistics are claimed.
